### Root cause of "cameras do not join one model": DATA, not pipeline
- No EXIF in any of the 11 PNGs; at least two pixel sizes (1672x941 majority, 1536x1024, and a third), i.e. different generators or crops. COLMAP had no focal prior (prior_focal=0, default 1.2*max(w,h)=2006 px) and every verified pair is config 3 (UNCALIBRATED).
- The connectivity graph is the cause. At >=30 RANSAC inliers (cv2 SIFT, independent of COLMAP) the 11 images form ONE group of 4 plus 7 isolated images; the 4 images COLMAP registered (ev_002, ev_004, ev_007, ev_010) are the strongest-linked cluster (scene-gate inliers 134-296). The control forms one 31-image component and registers 25.
- The mapper was retried with three settings (seed 0, seed 1, relaxed thresholds): 2, 2, 4 images registered, 32/58/80 triangulated points; median structure point seen by 2 cameras (needs >= 3).
- Scene-gate links that exist have inlier ratios of only 0.13-0.20: consistent with the views not being rigid-consistent images of one room, as visual inspection indicated. This is evidence, not proof of generation.
- Therefore, with this data no setting of this pipeline is expected to give a metric single-model room. Not verified: that real photographs of the same room would register (no such dataset was run).

### Pipeline defects: fixed and not fixed
- D1 FIXED: GPU never used. `ColmapConfig.use_gpu` defaulted False for a CUDA COLMAP 4.2.0, so extraction/matching ran on CPU. Now auto-detected (`detect_colmap_gpu`: banner reports CUDA and >= 1536 MB VRAM free), `RE_COLMAP_GPU=0/1` override; a CPU fallback records reason and effect in `last_run_info["gpu"]` and in this report. Affine+DSP SIFT extraction (default `robust_sift`) is CPU-only inside COLMAP and stays on CPU (recorded); matching moved to GPU. GPU matching is NOT verified bit-identical to CPU (room11 registered 6 vs 4 images between the CPU and GPU runs, though D2 also changed between them): the deterministic-mode claim (B12) is unverified for GPU matching.
- D2 FIXED: mixed pixel sizes disabled the shared-camera prior (`_uniform_image_size`), so every image got its own camera and COLMAP fitted focals of 15,607 / 1,231 / 3,072 / 1,090 px on the 4 registered cameras of room11. `adopt_solved_intrinsics` correctly refused disagreeing intrinsics, so depth and perception skipped. Same-size images now share one camera per size (`_size_folders` + `--ImageReader.single_camera_per_folder`). Effect on room11: intrinsics adopted (fx 788-1331 px, trust estimated), depth ran, perception ran. Registration stayed partial: the fix restores downstream stages, it cannot create overlap that is not in the data. Not tested on a real mixed-size capture.
- D3 NOT FIXED (hook needed in `apps/api/jobs.py::_scene_gate`, owned elsewhere): the LightGlue pair verifier appears to leave VRAM held before COLMAP starts (detector saw 3822 of 6141 MB free on a GPU that was empty before the run). It should drop the verifier and call `engine.core.device.release_vram()` before returning. Inferred, not isolated by measurement.
- D4 NOT FIXED: perception has no indoor/outdoor gate. On the exterior South Building control the open-vocabulary detector promoted 10 indoor classes (wardrobe, floor lamp, rug, curtain) as furniture entities and the layout stage produced 9 walls, a ceiling and a window from a facade. Control "success" for perception is therefore not evidence of correct perception.

### Why detections did not become positioned entities (room runs)
1. room11 CPU run: `perception` skipped "no trusted intrinsics -- lifting needs a camera model". Chain: mixed sizes -> per-image cameras -> disagreeing focals -> `adopt_solved_intrinsics` refuses -> `options.intrinsics=None` -> `depth` skipped -> no metric depth maps -> perception skipped. Fixed by D2.
2. After D2 (room11 GPU, idle): 67 masks (Grounding DINO boxes + SAM 2 masks, no mask fallback) -> 67 lifted -> 7 merged -> 7 entities (6 furniture); the rest rejected by the association gate (63 DIFFERENT_ENTITY, 4 SAME_ENTITY_CONFIRMED) and by dropped_detail reasons single_view / outside_scene. Only 6 of 11 cameras are registered, so 5 photographs contribute nothing. Positions are in a RELATIVE (unit-less) frame (scale_state=relative, no measured baseline), so mesh/dense/fusion are skipped ("would silently claim meters").
3. room6: no sparse model; the ladder fell back to 6 independent single-image hypotheses (84 entities) with "relative placement unknown": entities exist but are not positioned in a shared frame.
